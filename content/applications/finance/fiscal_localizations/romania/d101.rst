===============================================
D101 profit tax return and micro-enterprise tax
===============================================

The :guilabel:`Romania - D101 Profit Tax Return and Micro-enterprise Tax` (`l10n_ro_d101`) module
has two features:

- the **D101** (*Declarația privind impozitul pe profit*), the annual corporate income tax return, in
  the model of **OPANAF 206/2025** (reporting year 2025). It is computed from the accounts, validated
  and exported as the XML file filed with :abbr:`ANAF (Agenția Națională de Administrare Fiscală)`;
- a quarterly helper for the **micro-enterprise income tax** (*impozitul pe veniturile
  microîntreprinderilor*), which computes the tax base and the tax of the quarter and checks the
  eligibility thresholds. It does not produce a declaration.

This page describes the specific features of these two tools. The general flow of the Romanian
declarations (statuses, deadlines, DUK validation, filing) is described in :doc:`declarations`.

Configuration
=============

:ref:`Install <general/install>` the :guilabel:`Romania - D101 Profit Tax Return and
Micro-enterprise Tax` module. It installs the :doc:`financial statements <financial_statements>`
module as a dependency, and, through it, the Romanian accounting registers.

The income and expenses of the return are read from the **Romanian trial balance**
(*balanța de verificare*) of the accounting registers (see :doc:`statutory_registers`), the same
source as the financial statements: the D101, the micro-enterprise tax, the financial statements and
the trial balance therefore show the same figures. Only the posted journal entries count, and the
income and expense accounts are the accounts of classes 6 and 7 of the chart of accounts.

Check the company data of the :ref:`declarations <localizations/romania/declarations/configuration>`
(declarant, main CAEN code) before you start.

Profit tax return (D101)
========================

#. Go to :menuselection:`Accounting --> Romania (ANAF) --> Declarations --> D101 Profit Tax Return`
   and click :guilabel:`New`.
#. Choose the :guilabel:`Year`. The D101 is annual; the :guilabel:`Deadline` is the 25th of June of
   the following year (moved to the next working day). Select the :guilabel:`Profit Tax Type` if it
   is not the default `103` (*profit tax of Romanian legal persons*); the other types are `102`
   (prepayments of banks), `104` (annual profit tax of agriculture and non-profit organisations) and
   `105` (foreign legal persons).
#. In the :guilabel:`Header` tab, check the options of the return: :guilabel:`Exclude Result Closing
   Entries` (ticked by default: the entries that close the income and expense accounts into account
   121 are left out, so that the year can be computed before or after the closing), :guilabel:`Profit
   Tax Payer from Quarter` (for a micro-enterprise that became a profit tax payer during the year,
   the return then covers the period from the beginning of that quarter), the regularisation option
   of non-residents, and the options for a return filed after a cancellation of the verification
   reserve.
#. Click :guilabel:`Compute`. The :guilabel:`Rows` tab is filled in. The :guilabel:`Accounting Data`
   group of the :guilabel:`Header` tab shows the net turnover, the protocol expenses and the
   sponsorship expenses that were used.
#. Fill in the manual rows (see below), then click :guilabel:`Validate`, :guilabel:`Export XML`, file
   the return, and click :guilabel:`Mark as Submitted`, as described in :doc:`declarations`.

Rows: computed and manual
-------------------------

Every row of the :guilabel:`Rows` tab has a :guilabel:`Computed` value, an :guilabel:`Adjustment`
that you can type, and the resulting :guilabel:`Amount`. The adjustments are kept when the return is
recomputed. The manual rows are marked in blue.

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 25 75

   * - Rows
     - Source
   * - 1, 2, 4, 5
     - Net turnovers of the accounts of classes 6 and 7: row 1 operating income (accounts 70 to 75
       without 7418, plus 781 and 79), row 2 operating expenses (60 to 65 and 681, **plus** the profit tax
       expense 691, 694 and 697, as the instructions of the form require), row 4 financial income
       (76, 7418 and 786) and row 5 financial expenses (66 and 686). Rows 3, 6, 7 and 10 are totals.
   * - 23, 27, 28
     - Profit tax expense (691, 694, 697), sponsorship expenses (6582) and accounting depreciation
       (6811).
   * - 11
     - Proposed equal to row 28 (accounting depreciation); adjust it for the tax depreciation.
   * - 12.1
     - Proposed from row 31 of the previous return.
   * - 26
     - Protocol expenses (account 6231) above the limit of 2% of the base defined by the Fiscal Code.
   * - 39, 39.1
     - Row 39 is proposed from the previous return (losses not yet recovered, plus the loss of the
       previous year). Row 39.1, the loss recovered in the year, is limited to 70% of the profit
       before the recovery (row 38.1) and to row 39.
   * - 40, 40.1, 41, 41.1
     - Taxable profit, tax loss of the year, total profit tax and profit tax at 16%.
   * - 43.1
     - Sponsorship credit: the lowest of the sponsorship expenses, 0.75% of the net turnover and 20%
       of (row 41 - row 42), minus row 43.2.
   * - 48, 48.1, 48.2, 52, 53
     - Annual profit tax due and the difference to pay (row 52) or to recover (row 53), with the
       official formulas.
   * - All the other rows, including 46, 47, 49, 50 and 51
     - **Manual**: elements similar to income and expenses, deductions, non-taxable income,
       non-deductible expenses, tax credits, the minimum turnover tax, the tax established by the
       tax inspection and the prepayments declared with the form 100 (row 50). Type them in the
       :guilabel:`Adjustment` column.

Checks
------

The validation of the D101 verifies the sub-rows and the signs of the rows, the limits of the Fiscal
Code on the tax credits (rows 39.1, 42.1 to 45), the CAEN code, and the legal basis of a return
filed after a cancellation of the reserve. It also compares the accounting result of row 7 with the
**net result of the financial statements** of the same year (warning `D101_FS`): if they differ,
check the closing entries and the financial statements. The manual rows that you filled in are
listed in an information message.

Corrections and limits
----------------------

- A corrected return is created with :guilabel:`Create Rectificative`. For a return rectified after
  a conformity notice, tick :guilabel:`Rectification after a Conformity Notice`.
- The fiscal groups, the modified fiscal year, the dates of liquidation and the computation of the
  minimum turnover tax are not covered. The prepayments of banks (type `102`) can be selected, but
  their computation is left to you.
- The XSD schema that ANAF publishes for the D101 is not usable (it is not well-formed XML and
  describes an older version of the form). Odoo therefore checks the XML against a schema built from
  the official structure of the form: a valid :guilabel:`XSD Check` does not replace the
  **DUKIntegrator validation**, which is the reference. Use a configured
  :ref:`DUK validator service <localizations/romania/declarations/duk>`.
- The D101 is validated by DUKIntegrator without the PDF, because the PDF of the return contains
  the annexes of the declaration, which are assembled with the ANAF assistance program.

Micro-enterprise tax
====================

The micro-enterprise tax is declared and paid with the form **100 (D100)** by the 25th of the month
following each quarter. **Odoo does not generate the D100 or its XML**: the module computes the
amount of the quarter, and you enter it in the ANAF assistance program for the form 100.

Go to :menuselection:`Accounting --> Romania (ANAF) --> Declarations --> Micro-enterprise Tax` and
click :guilabel:`New`, then choose the :guilabel:`Year` and the :guilabel:`Quarter`. One computation
per company and quarter can exist.

#. Choose the :guilabel:`Tax Rate` (1% or 3%). The rates allowed depend on the year: in 2025, 1% or
   3%; from 2026, a single rate of 1%. Odoo does not choose the rate for you: for 2025, it shows a
   note when the cumulated base exceeds 60,000 EUR while the rate is 1%, so that you check whether the
   3% rate applies.
#. In the group :guilabel:`Eligibility`, tick :guilabel:`At Least One Employee` and
   :guilabel:`Shareholders Condition Met` once you have checked these two conditions of art. 47 of
   the Fiscal Code. Odoo cannot know them and reminds you in the notes until they are ticked.
#. Click :guilabel:`Compute`.

The computation works on the figures cumulated from 1 January. The cumulated **tax base** is the
revenue of class 7, minus the items that art. 53 (1) of the Fiscal Code excludes (variation of
stocks, own production of fixed assets, operating subsidies, reversals of provisions, foreign
exchange gains and dividends), plus the trade discounts received after invoicing (account 609), plus
the :guilabel:`Manual Adjustment (cumulated)` that you enter for the elements that cannot be
identified from the accounts (negative to deduct). The **tax of the quarter** is the cumulated base
minus the base of the previous quarters, multiplied by the rate. The detail of the deductions is
listed at the bottom of the form.

The **thresholds** are 250,000 EUR in 2025 and 100,000 EUR from 2026. The cumulated tax base (shown
as :guilabel:`Cumulated Revenue in EUR`) is converted with the exchange rate (EUR to RON) of 31
December of the previous year, taken from the currency rates of Odoo. If the threshold is exceeded,
the line is shown in red and a note explains that the company becomes a profit tax payer from that
quarter: file the D101 for the rest of the year with :guilabel:`Profit Tax Payer from Quarter` set
to that quarter. If there is no EUR rate at 31 December, the threshold cannot be checked and a note
says so.

When the amount is correct, click :guilabel:`Mark as Done`. Click :guilabel:`Create Journal Entry` to
create the accrual of the tax of the quarter (debit account 698, credit account 4418) in a
miscellaneous journal; the entry is dated at the end of the quarter and is created as a draft, which
you review and post. The accounts must exist in the chart of accounts. :guilabel:`Reset to Draft` is
refused while the entry is posted.

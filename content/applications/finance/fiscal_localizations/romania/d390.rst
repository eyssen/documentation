==================================
D390 VIES recapitulative statement
==================================

The :guilabel:`Romania - D390 Recapitulative Statement (VIES)` (`l10n_ro_d390`) module prepares the
**D390** (*Declarația recapitulativă privind livrările/achizițiile/prestările intracomunitare*),
the monthly statement of the intra-Community operations of the company with VAT payers of other EU
member states, in the model of **OPANAF 705/2020**. The operations are grouped by operation type and
by partner (country and VAT number), and exported as the XML file filed with
:abbr:`ANAF (Agenția Națională de Administrare Fiscală)`.

This page describes the specific features of the D390. The general flow of the Romanian
declarations (statuses, deadlines, DUK validation, filing) is described in
:doc:`declarations`.

Configuration
=============

:ref:`Install <general/install>` the :guilabel:`Romania - D390 Recapitulative Statement (VIES)`
module, which installs the :doc:`D300 <d300>` module as a dependency. The company settings are those
of the :ref:`declarations <localizations/romania/declarations/configuration>`: the declarant, the
company Tax ID and the other data of the section :guilabel:`Romanian ANAF Declarations` in
:menuselection:`Accounting --> Configuration --> Settings`.

The operation type of each operation comes from the **Romanian tax code** of the tax used on the
document (field :guilabel:`D390 Operation Type` of the tax code, in :menuselection:`Accounting -->
Romania (ANAF) --> Configuration --> Romanian Tax Codes`), or from a :guilabel:`Tax Mapping Rule` of
the form D390. For example, use a rule on a fiscal position to report the supplies of a triangular
operation with type `T`.

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 10 90

   * - Type
     - Operation
   * - `L`
     - Intra-Community supplies of goods
   * - `T`
     - Supplies within a triangular operation
   * - `A`
     - Intra-Community acquisitions of goods
   * - `P`
     - Intra-Community supplies of services
   * - `S`
     - Intra-Community acquisitions of services
   * - `R`
     - Supplies under the special scheme for farmers

Prepare the D390
================

#. Go to :menuselection:`Accounting --> Romania (ANAF) --> Declarations --> D390 VIES Statement`
   and click :guilabel:`New`.
#. Choose the :guilabel:`Year` and the :guilabel:`Month`. The D390 is monthly only; the
   :guilabel:`Deadline` is the 25th of the following month, moved to the next working day.
#. Choose how the credit notes of earlier months are handled in :guilabel:`Credit Notes of Earlier
   Periods` (see below).
#. Click :guilabel:`Compute`, and review the :guilabel:`Operations` tab.
#. Click :guilabel:`Validate`, then :guilabel:`Export XML`, file the statement, and click
   :guilabel:`Mark as Submitted`, as described in :doc:`declarations`.

How the operations are computed
-------------------------------

The statement is computed from the posted invoices and bills of the month, whose taxes have a D390
operation type. The :guilabel:`Operations` tab has one line per operation type, country, VAT number
and partner name, with the :guilabel:`Taxable Base` in whole lei. A line shows the number of
documents, and the list icon opens the journal items behind it.

- The VAT number of the partner is split into the VIES country prefix and the number
  (:guilabel:`VAT Number`, without the prefix). The prefix of Greece is `EL`; `XI` (Northern
  Ireland) is accepted for goods. The United Kingdom is not an EU member state: operations with a
  British partner are exports or imports, not D390 operations.
- The VAT number is mandatory for the types `L`, `T`, `P` and `R`.
- A partner that is not established in another EU member state, or without the mandatory VAT
  number, is reported with an error message (`D390_PARTNER`) naming the partner and the document.
  Check the country and the VAT number of the partner, or the tax used on the document. Operations
  of a partner outside the EU are not included in the statement.
- Credit notes reduce the base of the partner.

The totals of the statement (:guilabel:`Total L`, :guilabel:`Total T`, ... and the number of
operations) are shown below the lines. The statement is not due if there is no operation in the
month: the validation shows the error `D390_EMPTY`.

Credit notes of earlier periods
-------------------------------

A credit note issued in a month for an invoice of an earlier month can be reported in two ways. Set
the choice in :guilabel:`Credit Notes of Earlier Periods`:

:guilabel:`In the period of the credit note` (default)
   The credit note is reported as a **negative** base in the month in which it is issued. Odoo shows
   a warning for the lines with a negative base.

:guilabel:`Rectify the original period`
   The credit note is left out of the month and listed in a warning message (`D390_RECTIFY`) that
   gives the original period. File a **rectificative D390** for that month.

Rectificatives
--------------

Click :guilabel:`Create Rectificative` on an exported or submitted statement to create a new version
of the same month. The XML of a rectificative statement contains the **complete corrected list** of
operations, not only the differences, so every base must be positive: the validation fails if a
line has a base that is zero or negative.

Manual lines and call-off stock
-------------------------------

The lines of the :guilabel:`Operations` tab can be edited, and new lines can be added. A line that is
added or modified is flagged :guilabel:`Manual` and is kept when the statement is recomputed.

The :guilabel:`Call-off Stock` tab is filled in by hand. It declares the call-off stock arrangements
(art. 275¹ of the Fiscal Code): section `A` for the goods transferred to the intended acquirer
(:guilabel:`Country`, :guilabel:`VAT Number`, :guilabel:`Acquirer`), and section `B` for the changes
(:guilabel:`Reason`: return of the goods, replacement of the acquirer, other). When the acquirer is
replaced, give the :guilabel:`New Acquirer Country` and :guilabel:`New Acquirer VAT Number`. The
statement can be exported without call-off stock lines.

Checks
------

On top of the common checks, the validation of the D390 verifies that:

- each partner and operation type appears **once** (no duplicates), and the base is not zero;
- the country is an EU member state accepted by ANAF, and the VAT number is filled in for the types
  that require it;
- the totals agree with the **D300 of the same month** (warning `D390_D300`): type `L` with
  rows 1 and 2, `P` with rows 3.1 and 4, `A` with rows 5 and 6, and `S` with rows 7.1 and 8. The
  exempt intra-Community acquisitions (D300 row 29) explain a difference on `A` and `S`.

==========================
D394 informative statement
==========================

The :guilabel:`Romania - D394 Informative Statement (Domestic Supplies and Acquisitions)`
(`l10n_ro_d394`) module prepares the **D394** (*Declarația informativă privind livrările/prestările
și achizițiile efectuate pe teritoriul național*), which lists the domestic supplies and
acquisitions of the company per partner, operation type and VAT rate. The module follows the model
of **OPANAF 3769/2015 as replaced by OPANAF 2194/2025**, which applies to the operations from
**August 2025**. The statement is computed from the posted invoices, bills and credit notes of the
period, validated, and exported as the XML file filed with
:abbr:`ANAF (Agenția Națională de Administrare Fiscală)`.

This page describes the specific features of the D394. The general flow of the Romanian
declarations (statuses, deadlines, DUK validation, filing) is described in
:doc:`declarations`.

Configuration
=============

:ref:`Install <general/install>` the :guilabel:`Romania - D394 Informative Statement (Domestic
Supplies and Acquisitions)` module, which installs the :doc:`D300 <d300>` module as a dependency.
The statement is only available for companies whose fiscal country is Romania. If the *Point of
Sale* app is installed, its receipts are used for the cash register section.

Company data
------------

Fill in the company data of the :ref:`declarations <localizations/romania/declarations/configuration>`:
the declarant, the main CAEN code (mandatory), the phone number of the company (mandatory in the
D394), and, if the company has a fiscal representative, the representative. Then go to
:menuselection:`Accounting --> Configuration --> Settings`, section :guilabel:`Romanian ANAF
Declarations`, setting :guilabel:`Informative Statement D394`, and fill in the data of the person who
**prepares** the statement (*întocmit*):

- :guilabel:`D394 Prepared By`: :guilabel:`Legal person` (for example, an accounting firm) or
  :guilabel:`Individual`;
- :guilabel:`Preparer Name` and :guilabel:`Preparer CUI/CNP`: if they are empty, the declarant is
  used. The code is checked (control digit);
- :guilabel:`Preparer Capacity` (*calitate*, for example *expert contabil*): mandatory for a legal
  person;
- :guilabel:`Preparer Position`: for an individual, the position in the company (if empty, the
  position of the declarant is used);
- :guilabel:`D394: Partners May Consult Transactions`: the option by which every VAT-registered
  partner may consult, through the ANAF application, the transactions declared with it. Once given
  for a fiscal year, it cannot be withdrawn.

These values are copied to each new statement, where they can be changed (:guilabel:`Declarant` tab).

Master data
-----------

The quality of the D394 depends on the data of the partners, products and invoices.

Partners
   The :guilabel:`D394 Partner Type` of a contact (next to the CNP) decides in which part of the
   statement the partner is reported. If it is empty, Odoo derives it: a Romanian Tax ID with the
   `RO` prefix, or a VAT payer in the ANAF register, is type 1; a Romanian partner not registered
   for VAT, and individuals, are type 2; a company of another EU member state is type 3; a company
   outside the EU is type 4. Set the field only to override this rule. Individuals (type 2) need the
   CNP or, without a CNP, the country, city, county and street of the address.

Products and product categories
   The :guilabel:`D394 Goods Code` of a product (:guilabel:`General Information` tab) or of its
   category categorizes the goods and services of the domestic reverse charge (cereals by NC code,
   waste, wood, certificates, electricity, natural gas, buildings and land, investment gold, mobile
   phones, microprocessors, consoles, tablets and laptops), and the nature of the acquisitions from
   individuals (land, buildings, other goods, services). If the product has no code, the code of its
   closest category is used. The code is mandatory for the reverse charge operations (`V` and `C`)
   with Romanian partners and for the acquisitions from individuals without a code (`34` goods and
   `35` services are used by default); the codes for mobile phones, microprocessors and consoles are
   also detailed on the normal taxable operations.

Invoices
   In the :guilabel:`Accounting` group of the :guilabel:`Other Info` tab of the invoice,
   :guilabel:`D394 Document Type` tells
   whether the document is an invoice, a simplified invoice, a fiscal receipt qualifying as
   simplified invoice, a purchase slip (*borderou*), a sheet of the agricultural sales book, a
   contract or another document. Simplified invoices and receipts are reported in totals per VAT
   rate; purchase slips, sales book sheets and contracts document the acquisitions from individuals.
   :guilabel:`D394 Issued By` indicates who issued the document (the company, the customer or a
   third party on behalf of the company, or the company on behalf of the supplier: self-billing); for
   a third party, select the :guilabel:`D394 Issuer`. On vendor bills, :guilabel:`Supplier VAT on
   Payment (at posting)` records whether the supplier applied VAT on payment when the bill was
   posted, so that a later change of the supplier does not alter past statements.

Taxes
   The D394 treatment of a tax comes from its Romanian tax code (field :guilabel:`D394 Treatment`):
   *Taxable* (`L`, `A` or `AI`), *Domestic reverse charge* (`V` and `C`), *Special scheme* (`LS`
   and `AS`), or *Not reported in D394* (intra-Community operations, which belong to the
   :doc:`D390 <d390>`, exports, imports, exempt and non-taxable operations). Acquisitions from
   persons not registered for VAT are always reported as `N`.

Invoice ranges
   The D394 reports the ranges of invoice numbers allocated by internal decision. Create them in
   :menuselection:`Accounting --> Romania (ANAF) --> Configuration --> D394 Invoice Ranges`: the
   :guilabel:`Series`, the :guilabel:`From Number` and :guilabel:`To Number`, the journal, the
   :guilabel:`Allocated On` date and the :guilabel:`Used Until` date. Without a range, the statement
   uses the ranges actually issued and shows a warning.

Prepare the D394
================

#. Go to :menuselection:`Accounting --> Romania (ANAF) --> Declarations --> D394 Informative
   Statement` and click :guilabel:`New`.
#. Choose the :guilabel:`Period Type` (:guilabel:`Month`, :guilabel:`Quarter`, :guilabel:`Semester`
   or :guilabel:`Year`), the year and the period. The :guilabel:`Deadline` is the 30th of the month
   following the period, moved to the next working day.
#. Click :guilabel:`Compute`. A period before August 2025 can be computed, but a warning reminds you
   that it belongs to the previous model.
#. Review the tabs described below and complete the manual data.
#. Click :guilabel:`Validate`, then :guilabel:`Export XML`, file the statement, and click
   :guilabel:`Mark as Submitted`, as described in :doc:`declarations`.

The documents are selected by date: the **customer invoices and credit notes issued** in the period
(invoice date) and the **vendor bills received** in the period (accounting date), whatever the
exigibility of the VAT. The amounts are rounded to whole lei per operation. An invoice with several
rates is counted once, on the rate with the highest VAT.

What is in the statement
------------------------

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 22 78

   * - Tab
     - Content
   * - :guilabel:`Operations`
     - One line per partner, operation type and VAT rate (`L`, `LS`, `A`, `AI`, `AS`, `V`, `C`,
       `N`), with the partner type, the partner code, the number of invoices, the taxable base and
       the VAT. The document icon opens the invoices and the list icon opens the journal items.
       Below the lines, the number of partners of each type.
   * - :guilabel:`Summaries`
     - The totals per partner type and VAT rate, with the goods details of the reverse charge and
       of the acquisitions from individuals, and the totals per VAT rate, including the simplified
       invoices and the receipts. They are computed from the operations: click :guilabel:`Refresh
       Summaries` after editing the operations (the validation does it too).
   * - :guilabel:`Invoice Series`
     - The allocated ranges, the invoices issued in the period, and those issued by customers or
       third parties, then the credit notes, cancelled invoices and self-billed invoices.
   * - :guilabel:`Cash Registers`
     - The monthly totals of the receipts of the fiscal cash registers (point of sale orders that
       were not invoiced) and the :guilabel:`Listed Activities`.
   * - :guilabel:`Other Information`
     - The VAT on payment amounts, the special schemes, the refund of the negative VAT balance and
       the nature of the operations that generate it.
   * - :guilabel:`Declarant`
     - The legal representative, the preparer and the consent option.

Automatic and manual data
-------------------------

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 35 65

   * - Computed from the accounting
     - Operations, summaries, the amounts of the simplified invoices and receipts per VAT rate (from
       the :guilabel:`D394 Document Type` of the documents), invoice series, credit notes, cancelled
       and self-billed invoices, cash register totals from the point of sale orders (when
       installed), the VAT on payment amounts of the period, the nature of the operations of the
       refund block, and, from the D300 of the same period, the refund request.
   * - Entered by hand
     - Cash register lines that do not come from point of sale orders (type `I2`, and type `I1`
       if the receipts are not recorded in the point of sale app), the
       :guilabel:`Listed Activities`, the :guilabel:`Special Schemes`, the box
       :guilabel:`Operations with Affiliated Persons`, and any correction of the computed lines.

All the sections can be edited. A line that is added or modified is flagged :guilabel:`Manual` and
is kept when the statement is recomputed.

Data to handle manually
-----------------------

Listed activities (CAEN)
   The :guilabel:`Listed Activities` table (tab :guilabel:`Cash Registers`) is the list of CAEN
   activities of the form for which the supplies are declared separately. It is never filled in
   automatically: Odoo does not know which of your supplies belong to these activities. If the
   company carries out one of them, add a line for each combination of activity, :guilabel:`VAT
   Rate` and :guilabel:`Operation` (supplies of goods or of services), with the
   :guilabel:`Value of Supplies` and the :guilabel:`VAT`. The activities available in the list are
   `1071` pastry and confectionery, `4520` car wash, `4730` retail sale of automotive fuel, `47761`
   flowers, plants and seeds, `47762` pets and pet food, `4932` taxi, `55101` hotels, `55102`
   guesthouses, `56103` restaurants, `5630` beverage serving, `812` cleaning, `9313` fitness,
   `9602` hairdressing and beauty treatment, and `9603` funeral activities. Each combination can be
   declared only once.

Special schemes
   The operations under the margin schemes are not computed. The taxes with the treatment *Special
   scheme* put the operations in the types `LS` and `AS` (with VAT rate 0), but the figures of the
   schemes are typed in the group :guilabel:`Special Schemes` of the tab :guilabel:`Other
   Information`: for **travel agencies**, the amount paid by the travellers, the costs, the profit
   margin and the VAT, and for **second-hand goods**, the sale price, the purchase price, the profit
   margin and the VAT.

Cash registers and refund
   The cash register lines (types `I1` and `I2`) give, per month, the number of cash registers
   (*AMEF*), the number of fiscal receipts, the total of the receipts, and the base and VAT per
   rate. For `I1`, the number of cash registers and of receipts is mandatory. In the refund block,
   the nature of the operations that generate the negative VAT balance is derived from the
   accounting; check it when the refund is requested.

Checks
------

In addition to the common checks, the validation of the D394 verifies, among others, that:

- the CAEN code, the phone number, the legal representative and the preparer are filled in, and
  their codes are valid;
- every partner has a valid code (CUI for type 1, the VAT number for type 3) and appears only once
  per type and rate, and the individuals without CNP have an address;
- the reverse charge operations and the acquisitions from individuals have a goods code;
- when supplies are declared, the invoice series of type 1 (allocated ranges) and of type 2
  (invoices issued in the period) are present; without a configured range, Odoo falls back to the
  ranges actually issued and shows a warning;
- the cash register amounts are positive, the receipts are not lower than the bases, and the months
  are within the period;
- the operations agree with the **D300 of the same period** (warnings `D394_D300`): for example,
  `L` at 21% and 11% with rows 9 and 10, `V` with row 13, `C` with rows 12.1 and 12.2, and `A` with
  rows 24 and 25. Differences are expected for the VAT on payment operations, which the D300
  reports when the VAT becomes chargeable.

Corrections
-----------

A corrected D394 is a **complete new statement** that replaces the one filed. Click
:guilabel:`Create Corrected Declaration` on an exported or submitted statement: Odoo creates the next
version of the period as a draft copy of the statement, with its lines. Click :guilabel:`Compute` to
refresh it from the accounting: the lines flagged :guilabel:`Manual` are kept. The XML has no
rectification flag.

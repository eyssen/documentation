==============
Wire transfers
==============

The **Wire Transfer** payment method allows you to provide payment instructions to your customers,
such as your bank details and a reference to include with the payment. These instructions are
displayed after the customer selects :guilabel:`Wire Transfer` as the payment method and clicks the
:guilabel:`Pay now` button at the end of the checkout process on your ecommerce website or
on the customer portal.

.. screenshot:: finance-payment-providers-wire-transfer-payment-instructions-portal
   :menu: (customer portal) ‣ an order ‣ Pay now ‣ Wire Transfer
   :shows: The customer portal after choosing Wire Transfer: the "Payment instructions" box with the bank account number, the amount and the communication reference to include with the transfer.
   :highlight: The communication reference.
   :data: Order S00042, bank account BE12 3456 7890 1234, reference S00042.
   :module: payment_custom
   :notes: English UI, light theme, 1440px width.

.. note::
   - While this method is widely accessible and requires minimal setup, it is not efficient
     process-wise. It is recommended to set up a :doc:`payment provider <../payment_providers>`
     instead.
   - Online orders remain in the :guilabel:`Quotation sent` (i.e., unpaid order) stage until you
     receive the payment and manually :guilabel:`Confirm` the order.

.. tip::
   **Wire transfer** can be used as a template for other payment methods that are processed
   manually, such as checks, by renaming or duplicating it.

Configuration
=============

To configure **Wire Transfer**:

#. :ref:`Navigate to the Wire Transfer payment provider <payment_providers/supported_providers>`.
#. In the :guilabel:`Configuration` tab, select whether the memo or :guilabel:`Communication` to be
   displayed alongside the payment instructions should be:

   - :guilabel:`Based on Document Reference`: the sales order or invoice number
   - :guilabel:`Based on Customer ID`: the customer identifier

#. Enable :guilabel:`Enable QR Codes` to activate QR code payments.

   .. note::
      :doc:`Additional accounting setup <../accounting/customer_invoices/epc_qr_code>` is required
      to use QR codes.

#. Define the payment instructions in the :guilabel:`Messages` tab. If :doc:`a bank account
   <../accounting/bank>` has already been defined, the account number is automatically added to the
   default message generated by Odoo. You can also add it afterward and update the message by
   clicking :guilabel:`Reload Pending Message`.

   .. screenshot:: finance-payment-providers-wire-transfer-payment-instructions
      :menu: Accounting ‣ Configuration ‣ Payment Providers ‣ Wire Transfer ‣ Messages tab
      :shows: The Messages tab of the Wire Transfer payment provider with the "Pending Message" HTML editor containing the default payment instructions including the bank account number, and the "Reload pending message" link.
      :highlight: The "Pending Message" field.
      :data: Bank account BE12 3456 7890 1234 configured on the Bank journal.
      :module: payment_custom
      :notes: English UI, light theme, 1440px width.

#. Set the :guilabel:`State` field to :guilabel:`Enabled`.

.. tip::
   You can also test wire transfers using the :ref:`payment_providers/test-mode`.

Payment status page
===================

After the customer confirms a wire transfer payment, the transaction stays in the
:guilabel:`Pending` state until you receive the money. Odoo normally shows a short *payment status*
page (`/payment/status`) that forwards the customer to the order confirmation (or the portal
document) once the transaction has been processed.

With the *Payment Custom — Skip Status Page* (`payment_custom_skip_status`) module installed, the
customer is redirected **directly** to the confirmation page after choosing :guilabel:`Wire
Transfer` (or any other manual payment method based on it), without passing through the status
page. The redirection happens on the server, so it works even when the website's JavaScript is
blocked or outdated. The pending transaction is then post-processed automatically by the scheduled
action :guilabel:`Payment: Post-process transactions` within a few minutes, exactly as when a
customer clicks the :guilabel:`Skip` link of the status page. Online payment providers and
cancelled or failed payments are not affected.

.. note::
   This behavior requires the *Payment Custom — Skip Status Page* module; it has no settings.

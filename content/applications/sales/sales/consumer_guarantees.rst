===================
Consumer guarantees
===================

Since 27 September 2026, traders who sell goods to consumers in the European Union have to inform
them about the legal guarantee of conformity with an official, harmonised notice. The European
Commission publishes this notice in all 24 official languages of the EU, and it must be shown
without any change.

The **Consumer Guarantees** app shows the harmonised notice wherever a consumer decides to buy: on
the website, at checkout, in the customer portal, on payment links, in order documents and, as a
printed poster, in the store.

.. important::
   The app helps you to meet these information duties, but it does not make your business
   compliant on its own. The rules differ between Member States, and some duties are not covered
   yet (see below). Have your setup checked by your legal counsel.

What is covered
===============

- The **harmonised notice on the legal guarantee** in all 24 official EU languages, taken
  unchanged from the official files of the European Commission.
- A legal guarantee page on the website and notice links in the website footer, on product pages,
  in the cart, at the payment step and on the order confirmation page.
- A notice section in the customer portal, and notice links in the portal's sign and pay windows
  and on payment links.
- A notice page at the end of consumer quotations, sales orders and pro-forma invoices.
- A notice poster for the store in A4, A3, A2 or A1, in colour or in black and white, and a log of
  where and when the poster was put up.
- Reference data for the EU Member States, the EEA countries and the outermost regions of the EU,
  used to decide where the notice applies and in which languages it is printed.

What is not covered yet
=======================

- The **harmonised label** for a commercial guarantee of durability offered by the producer (GARAN
  label).
- Guarantee information received from producers and suppliers, and statements passed on to
  resellers.
- Information on reparability, spare parts, software updates and digital content or services.
- Point of Sale, printed catalogues and screens in the store.

Where the notice applies
========================

The notice is enabled automatically for companies located in an EU Member State, including the
outermost regions such as Réunion or Guadeloupe. For companies located elsewhere, for example in
Norway or in the United Kingdom, it is disabled by default.

Quotations, orders, the customer portal and payment links show the notice to consumers buying
goods (on the website, the links appear for goods whatever the customer type):

- A customer is a consumer when their :guilabel:`Consumer Guarantee Customer Type` is
  :guilabel:`Consumer`, or when it is :guilabel:`Automatic` and the customer is not a company. Set
  :guilabel:`Business` for companies and sole traders that buy for their business.
- A product counts as goods when its :guilabel:`Consumer Goods Type` is :guilabel:`Goods` or
  :guilabel:`Goods with digital elements`. Services never trigger the notice. For a combo product,
  the products it contains are checked.

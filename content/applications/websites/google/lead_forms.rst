================
Google Ads leads
================

With **lead form assets**, Google Ads collects the contact details of interested people inside the
ad (Search, YouTube, Discover, Gmail), without them visiting your website. The Google app receives
these leads **in real time** in the CRM, and reports the progress of the deals back to Google Ads, so
that your campaigns learn which leads become customers.

Both features are in the :guilabel:`Google Ads Leads` block of :menuselection:`Google -->
Configuration --> Settings`, which is available when the CRM is installed.

.. _google/leads/webhook:

Lead form webhook
=================

#. In the Odoo settings, enable :guilabel:`Lead Form Webhook`. Odoo displays:

   .. list-table::
      :header-rows: 1
      :widths: 30 70

      * - Field
        - Description
      * - :guilabel:`Webhook URL`
        - The address that receives the leads of this website. Copy it.
      * - :guilabel:`Key`
        - A secret key generated by Odoo (administrators only). Deliveries with another key are
          refused. :guilabel:`Generate New Key` replaces it: update Google Ads afterwards.
      * - :guilabel:`Sales Team` and :guilabel:`Salesperson`
        - The team and the salesperson of the new leads. They default to the ones of the website
          forms.
      * - :guilabel:`Ignore Test Leads`
        - Does not create the test leads sent from Google Ads. Otherwise they are created with the
          tag `Google Ads test lead`.

#. In Google Ads, open your **lead form asset**, go to *Lead delivery option*, choose *Webhook
   integration*, and paste the :guilabel:`Webhook URL` and the :guilabel:`Key`.
#. Click *Send test data* in Google Ads. The test lead appears in the CRM.

Each delivery creates one lead (a repeated delivery of the same lead is ignored):

- The title is `Google Ads lead: <name>`; the contact name, email, phone, company, job title, and
  address come from the answers of the form; the other answers are listed in the notes.
- The source is `Google Ads`, the medium is `lead_form`, and the campaign is the one of the ad.
- The lead is a *lead* or an *opportunity* depending on the sales team, and carries the Google Ads
  lead ID and the click identifier (in the :guilabel:`Ad Tracking` tab, visible in developer mode).
- The person gave the data to you in the ad: no cookie consent is needed.

.. _google/leads/offline:

CRM offline conversions
=======================

Enable :guilabel:`CRM Offline Conversions` in the same block to upload the lead events to Google Ads
with the Data Manager API, for example *qualified*, *won*, or *lost* leads:

#. Fill in the :ref:`Google Ads account <google/offline/account>` (customer ID, manager account) and
   select the :doc:`Google connection <connection>`.
#. In Google Ads, create a conversion action of type **Import > Track conversions from clicks** per
   stage that you want to report, and copy its numeric ID.
#. Open the :doc:`lead stage rules </applications/sales/crm/optimize/lead_tracking>`
   (:menuselection:`CRM --> Configuration --> Lead Tracking Rules`, or the :guilabel:`Lead Stage
   Rules` button), and enter the :guilabel:`Google Ads Conversion Action ID` on the rule.

When a lead reaches the stage of the rule, the event is sent with the value of the rule, linked to the
click (`gclid`, `gbraid`, or `wbraid`) stored on the lead, or to the lead's contact details. Leads from
your website forms keep the identifiers of the visit; leads from the lead form webhook keep the Google
lead ID.

.. note::
   By default, a rule only applies to the **tracked leads**, the ones that carry an ad click identifier.
   Other leads cannot be attributed to a campaign.

The conversions of the same rule are also sent to GA4 when the :doc:`Measurement Protocol
<measurement_protocol>` is enabled.

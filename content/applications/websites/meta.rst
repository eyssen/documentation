====
Meta
====

The **Meta** app connects your website, your shop, your CRM, and your point of sale to Facebook and
Instagram. All its settings are in one place: :menuselection:`Meta --> Configuration --> Settings`.

.. cards::

   .. card:: Meta Pixel
      :target: meta/pixel

      Measure the visits and purchases in Meta Ads Manager, with the consent of the visitors.

   .. card:: Conversions API
      :target: meta/conversions_api

      Send purchases, leads, store sales, and CRM stages from the server, deduplicated with the pixel.

   .. card:: Connection and webhooks
      :target: meta/connection

      Connect a Meta account with your own app or a broker, and receive Meta notifications.

   .. card:: Product catalog
      :target: meta/catalog

      Publish your products in a Meta catalog, with real-time price and stock updates.

   .. card:: Lead Ads and Conversion Leads
      :target: meta/lead_ads

      Create CRM leads from Facebook and Instagram lead forms and report their stages to Meta.

   .. card:: Facebook and Instagram inbox and posts
      :target: meta/social

      Answer messages in Discuss and publish posts on your pages and Instagram accounts.

   .. card:: WhatsApp
      :target: meta/whatsapp

      Send templates and answer customers on WhatsApp with the official Cloud API.

The Meta app is based on the :doc:`shared tracking base </applications/websites/website/tracking/overview>`:
read the pages about :doc:`consent </applications/websites/website/tracking/cookie_consent>`,
:doc:`privacy </applications/websites/website/tracking/privacy>`, and :doc:`testing
</applications/websites/website/tracking/troubleshooting>`.
